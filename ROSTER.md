# Wild Arena roster: kits

Goal (owner): every animal has an identity as distinct as a fighting-game roster — a role, a signature move you can
see on the field, a clear strength and a clear weakness — while staying recognizably real. Values are the measured
swing (seconds of survival gained by moving it from their side to yours, `price_abs.js`; prices and values synced
from sim.js after the prehistoric batch 1 repricing, `abs_p1`, Oct 27, 2026, branch `animals-prehist1`; map-limited items
measured on their own maps: tundra items on 240 tundra boards, the bear and Utahraptor on 480 boards of their two maps).

Status: **strong** = distinct and worth picking; **thin** = real but blurs with others or rarely matters;
**weak** = barely matters on the field. Thin/weak ones are the next design targets.

## Gaps to work on (next design passes)
1. **Still weak in value (distinct now, but cheap filler by numbers): honey badger, hyena (solo), black bear, wolf (solo),
   jaguar, ostrich.** Ideas that remain (realistic):
   - Honey badger: the musk works but predators rarely go for an 11 kg badger (0.06/fight). A decoy pull (bitten
     enemies switch to it) would make it fire; it already holds 25 s grudges.
   - Lone hyena: alone it rarely lands enough bites for the limp to matter; the clan is where it shows.
   - Black bear: the bouncer mostly rattles your enemies' small predators; maybe let it bluff whatever comes for you.
   - Lone wolf: or simply only appear as a pack item (the tester role is clearer in packs: 0.5–1.2 chases/fight).
   - Jaguar: the water ambush only exists on wet maps (~half of them); a dry-map hook is still missing.
   - Ostrich: evasive getaway mount (tried before: neutral), plus lookout; the claw kick is visible but small.
   - Cougar: rider-snatcher bonus exists; the nape bite fires 0.12/fight (it needs prey not facing it).
2. **Giraffe and ostrich** (now fight like other herbivores on the other side, Oct 2026): but their value must come from their support role
   (lookout radius, getaway mount) being visible and strong enough.
3. **Cats (Oct 26 pass):** tiger (ambush + big holds), lion (sprint, coalition, leaps on the back of busy big prey),
   leopard (hidden guard, trees), jaguar (armor piercing, swims in unseen), cougar (12 m leap, nape bite from behind,
   riders), sabre-tooth (pin and stab). Signatures fire 0.1–0.6/fight; more would read better.
4. **Identity check in the audit** (done: `sig()` counters, `audit.js` SIGNATURES): count each signature per fight (sweeps, mock charges, stomps, tree drops,
   death rolls, holds, grudge kills, lookout spots, …) so we can see every animal actually does its thing.

## Cats on your side (Oct 2026 pass)
Owner: "cats murk me but I get little value out of them." Measured cause: guard cats chased the biggest threat
(often a tonne-plus herbivore) and died (86% of them per fight), and the other side's herbivores single out
predators. Changes (all realistic, all symmetric where they apply):
- Guard cats pick fights they can win: other predators, things their size, above all whatever is after you.
- A cat out of the action for ~8 s with nothing after it can ambush again.
- Cats sidestep and give ground when a much bigger herbivore comes for them.
Result, value on your side: lion 8 → ~20 s, jaguar 5 → ~19, cougar 7 → ~19, leopard 2 → ~4–11, tiger ~15
(unchanged within noise). Their biggest value is still denial: left on the other side they hunt you.
