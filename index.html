Terrain

The other side is one team, and the pressure builds over time. Predators hunt you and your guards; herbivores charge intruders. Break their morale and animals bolt out of the arena. After 30 seconds a fire, flood or blizzard closes in from the edges and pushes everyone together. You score by how long you stay alive, up to five minutes.

Progressive run prototype

Seven rounds up the price ladder: round 1 is all $100 animals (honey badgers, boars, leopards...), each round lets pricier animals onto the board, and the giants come at the end. Your survivors carry their wounds and scars, earn XP and level up. From round 3 one premium enemy joins the other side. Every round brings fresh money; you can heal, sell or buy. Two retries for the whole run.

5 in a Row

Today's five boards, the same for everyone. Draft a full $1,200 team for each and survive to 5:00 on every one; one retry for the whole run.

Simulation lab

One battle is a story. Hundreds are evidence. Run your draft across every terrain, or let random drafts price each animal by how many seconds of life it actually buys you.

Your draft, 200 battles

Price the board

Plays 480 battles with random affordable drafts (rotating terrain, current rules, riding the tallest animal drafted). For each animal it compares average time alive when it's on your team against when it isn't. That gap counts the animal twice: once for helping you, once for not being against you. The ± is one standard error.
